# Alex Masidlover

Technical Director | Software and Systems Engineering

I'm an engineer by background and inclination. I originally studied Electrical and Electronic Engineering at UMIST (including a year studying in France), followed by a PhD at the University of Manchester in semiconductor device simulation.

In 2005 I founded Zednax, a software development company which subsequently merged with Medoc Computers, where I'm now Technical Director.

My work encompasses software design and development, infrastructure, information security and the rather less deterministic problem of managing a team and the people within it.

I'm particularly interested in how we build and maintain software systems that remain useful, secure and economically viable over extended periods. I tend to favour open standards, automation and relatively simple solutions to complicated problems.

## Technical interests

- Software, Firmware and Hardware architecture and application development
- Infrastructure as code and configuration management
- Linux, networking and information security
- Cross-platform software development
- Database design and distributed systems
- The practical application of AI to software engineering and business operations
- Maintaining and modernising long-lived commercial software systems

I've worked with a fairly diverse range of languages and technologies over the years from COBOL and other 4GLs right through to current C#. I generally regard programming languages as tools rather than affiliations.

## Projects

- **Zymonic**: An application development framework developed around the requirements of real-world commercial software.
- **CoachStopWatch**: A cross-platform application for athletics coaches, developed to solve some of the practical problems I encounter whilst coaching.

## Writing

I intend to use this site to document some of the more interesting technical problems I encounter, the solutions that have worked and, potentially more usefully, some of the ones that haven't.

I'm also interested in the intersection between engineering, business management and human behaviour.

## Outside software

I'm an endurance running coach at Manchester Harriers & AC, as well as a runner, cyclist, hiker and climber (general outdoor enthusiast).

I'm involved in several voluntary and community organisations.

I also have a tendency to acquire engineering projects outside work, which is probably why I rarely find myself short of things to do.
